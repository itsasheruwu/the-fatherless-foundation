01 / The purpose

Help with the
day-to-day.

Clothing, school supplies, and bills can be a lot for one parent to manage.

We want to help single mothers cover those needs so they have more time and money for their children.

We’re still deciding how that support will work. Families’ needs should guide those decisions.

Our faith

Love that
shows up.

Jesus calls us to love our neighbors with compassion, generosity, and care.

That is the heart behind The Fatherless Foundation. We want families to feel seen, valued, and supported—not judged or left to carry everything alone.

Our plans are still taking shape, but our hope is simple: to reflect the love of Christ through practical help for single mothers and their children.

02 / The plan

The support
we have in mind.

These are our proposed areas of work. We need to arrange funding and decide how to provide help before opening any programs.

01

Everyday supplies

We hope to provide children’s clothing and school supplies. The items we offer will depend on what families need and what we can source.

02

Financial help

We’re considering help with household costs. We still need to decide who would qualify, what we could cover, and how payments would work.

03

People to count on

We want to bring together people who can give their time or help collect supplies. Volunteer roles have not been set up yet.

03 / Our story

Why we’re
starting this.

We’re David, Vandin, and Matt.

All three of us grew up without a father. It was hard on us and on our families, and we know how much it can take to get through that.

That experience led us to try to start something that could make a difference for other families. The Fatherless Foundation is where we’re beginning.

04 / Questions

Before you
get involved.

We’re still in planning. Here’s what that means for families and anyone who wants to help.

What is The Fatherless Foundation?+

We’re developing a Christian-led organization to help single mothers and children growing up without a father. Our plans include supplies and financial help. We have not opened programs yet.

What role does faith play?+

Our faith in Jesus shapes why we care and how we hope to serve: with compassion, dignity, and practical love for families.

Can families get help right now?+

Not yet. We haven’t decided on eligibility or where we’ll offer support. We are not accepting requests for help.

Can I donate or volunteer?+

We’re not taking donations or volunteer applications yet. We’ll post details here when those are open.

Is this a registered nonprofit?+

We have not established registered nonprofit or tax-exempt status. We are not accepting donations or offering tax deductions.

Are any partnerships confirmed?+

We have no confirmed partnerships to announce.
